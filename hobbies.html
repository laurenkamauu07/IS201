The Hobbies of Homer Simpson

From Duff beer tasting to backyard barbecue experiments, here are some activities Homer might enjoy in Springfield.

Homer's Favorite Pastimes

  • Watching sports on television
  • Eating donuts and trying new flavors
  • Barbecuing in the backyard
  • Fishing with Bart
  • Working on his car at home
  • Building and testing unusual gadgets

A Day in the Life: A Fun Plan

  1. Start the morning with a fresh donut and a strong cup of coffee.
  2. Spend the afternoon relaxing with a game or a classic baseball match.
  3. Try a new backyard recipe for dinner.
  4. Finish the evening by watching a silly, entertaining video.

Homer Hobby Ideas

These hobbies balance Homer’s love of comfort with a bit of adventurous fun:

  • Home brewing a custom soda or beer-style drink (non-alcoholic for safety)
  • Learning to cook a signature burger recipe
  • Painting funny portraits of his family
  • Exploring local Springfield landmarks with the family
